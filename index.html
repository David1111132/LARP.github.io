In-Game Rules (ERLC)

Welcome to Connecticut State Roleplay! (Note: Site name is Los Angeles Roleplay - please adjust rules text if needed)
Below, we will go over some general rules that you must follow while roleplaying.

First of all, We aren't a craaaazy strict community, we love having fun, roleplaying and having a good time doing so, but to ensure to have a great time, we all have to follow some rules.

No.1 - RDM: (Random Death Match)
Please do not shoot at random players while roleplaying, you wouldn't like that happening to you right? ... RIGHT?

No.2 - VDM: (Vehicle Death Match)
Please do not shoot or ram your car into random players while roleplaying.

No.3 - FRP: (Fail Roleplay)
Please maintain roleplays similar to real life. Dont bunny hop, or some other actions that may seem unrealistic; Not accepting a detain request, Cuff Rushing, Auto Jailing, etc...

No.4 - NLR: (New Life Rule)
If you have passed away, you should not remember any actions in your past life. If you're an LEO, you MAY respond to the same call as long as you dont bring up arguments about your past life.

No.5 - Common Sense:
There's a LOT of other rules. Please, do not search any loopholes and use common sense, adhere to your knowledge of RP. If you do not know if something is allowed, feel free to ask to one of our staff! They will be happy to assist you.
